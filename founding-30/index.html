What We Are

What Is
Strata Strength Lab?

Strata is a structured strength and athletic development facility for kids and teens ages 3–18. It's not a gym where kids show up and use equipment on their own.

Every session is coached, progressive, and built around what your child actually needs — real strength, body control, coordination, and the kind of confidence that shows up outside the gym too.

Whether your child plays sports, needs more structure, or just needs a better outlet for their energy — Strata is built to give you something you can actually see progress in.

Built for Combat Sports

Not Every Strength Program
Gets Kids Ready for the Mat.

If your child trains jiu-jitsu, wrestling, judo, or MMA — or is thinking about it — generic strength training isn't enough. Combat sports place unique demands on the body: explosive hip power, grip endurance, neck and trunk stability, the ability to generate force from awkward positions under real pressure.

Most programs don't train for any of that. Strata does. The strength system here is built by coaches who come from the mat — and who understand that the goal isn't just a stronger kid. It's a kid who is harder to take down, harder to hold, and harder to stop.

Specifically built to develop athletes in:

Brazilian Jiu-JitsuGrip strength, hip escape, guard retention, pressure resistance
WrestlingExplosive takedowns, scramble strength, bottom-position power
JudoGrip fighting, kuzushi, core stability, explosive entry
MMAFull-body conditioning, sport-specific power, injury resilience

Darrell Thompson and the Strata coaching team come directly from the combat sports world. This isn't strength training adapted for fighters — it's strength training designed for them from the ground up.
