Party Questions

Birthday Party FAQ

How long is the party?
Each party lasts 2 hours.
How many kids are included?
The Standard Party includes up to 12 kids. The Premium Party includes up to 18 kids.
What if I have more than 12 kids?
Extra kids are $15 each. Once you go over 12 kids, a second coach is also required at an additional $75.
Why do I need another coach?
Extra coaches help us keep the party safe, supervised, and fun for every kid in the group.
Can I add more time?
Yes. You can add 30 extra minutes for $50.
Can I add a Nerf battle?
Yes. The Nerf Battle Add-On is $50 and is coach-led.
