Questions

Open Mat FAQ

Who is Open Mat for?
Open Mat is best for athletes who are already enrolled in at least one regular class and want additional practice time. It is not the best first introduction to Strata. A free trial class in a structured program is a better starting point.
Is there a coach at Open Mat?
Yes. Strata coaches are present during all Open Mat sessions to supervise, assist, and provide guidance. Athletes are not left on their own.
Can my child attend Open Mat without a regular class membership?
Contact us to discuss the best option for your athlete. We want every child training with the right structure for their current level.
What equipment is available during Open Mat?
All of it. The full obstacle course, climbing wall, and strength training equipment are available during Open Mat hours. Check the schedule for specific Open Mat times.
