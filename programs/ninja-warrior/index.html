What Your Child Actually Gains

After 8 to 12 weeks of structured obstacle training, here is what parents consistently see.

💪

Grip & Upper Body Strength

Traversing obstacles builds real pulling and gripping strength that carries into every sport and daily activity.

⚖️

Balance & Body Control

Moving across varied obstacles trains proprioception and spatial awareness. These skills prevent injuries and improve athleticism.

🧠

Problem-Solving Under Pressure

🏆

Earned Confidence

The Method

Not Free Play. Coached Progression.

Every session follows a structure. Here is how your child progresses.

01

Movement Warm-Up

Every session starts with a structured warm-up that prepares joints, activates muscles, and sets the tone for focused work.

02

Skill Work

Coaches introduce or reinforce a specific obstacle skill: brachiation, balance beam traversal, lache technique, or grip endurance. Proper progressions are built in for each age group.

03

Obstacle Course Circuit

Athletes apply skills in a timed or structured circuit. Progressions are built in so every child is challenged at the right level. No one gets thrown into the deep end.

04

Cool-Down & Review

Sessions close with mobility work and a brief coach review so every athlete leaves knowing exactly what they worked on and what to aim for next time.

Questions

Ninja Warrior Training FAQ

Does my child need prior experience?
No experience needed. All athletes start with foundational movements and progress at their own pace. Coaches scale every challenge to the individual.
What age can my child start?
We work with toddlers as young as 2 through teens up to 18. Classes are grouped by age and ability so every athlete trains with peers at the right level.
Is Ninja Warrior training safe for young kids?
Yes. Our obstacle course progressions are specifically designed for young athletes with appropriate challenge levels at every age. Coaches supervise every movement.
How is this different from a gymnastics or PE class?
Ninja Warrior obstacle training at Strata is coached and progressive, not recreational. Every session has a structured plan, measurable progressions, and a movement quality standard. It is athletic development, not free play. If you are searching for gymnastics-adjacent movement for kids in Oroville, Strata is a great option: we develop balance, coordination, climbing, and obstacle skills in a fun, coach-led environment without the competitive gymnastics structure.
What should my child wear?
Comfortable athletic clothes and athletic shoes (or grip socks). No sandals or open-toed shoes. No jewelry on the obstacle course.
How often should my child train?
2 to 3 times per week is ideal for consistent progress. Once per week maintains skills. We offer flexible scheduling to fit your family.
