Forge Protocol / Sports Attribute Development

The Forge Protocol is Strata's structured strength and athletic development system. Built around the foundational movement patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull, and carry. It applies discipline, repetition, and progressive overload to develop real-world functional strength. Sports attribute development layers on top: speed mechanics, explosive power, agility, and multi-directional conditioning that transfers directly to game day performance. This is not a circuit class or generic gym workout. It is a system where movement quality comes first, load and intensity are earned, and every athlete leaves more capable than when they walked in.

How Athletes Progress

The Strata Levels System

Every athlete progresses through the Strata system at their own pace. No shortcuts, no ceiling.

Level 1

Foundation

Movement fundamentals, body awareness, and coordination basics. This is where every athlete starts. Regardless of age or experience.

Level 2

Development

Progressive loading, skill refinement, and increasing challenge. Athletes here have the basics locked in and are building real strength.

Level 3

Performance

Advanced programming, sport-specific attributes, and competition-level preparation. Earned through consistent work at levels 1 and 2.

Common Questions

Programs FAQ

What is the Forge Protocol?
The Forge Protocol is Strata's structured strength training system. It is built around the foundational movement patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull, carry. Applied with progressive overload and a strict movement quality standard. Strength is earned through structure and repetition, not shortcuts.
Is Ninja Warrior obstacle training just for older kids?
No. The obstacle course system at Strata serves toddlers through teens. The progressions scale to meet each child where they are. Every age group benefits from developing coordination, grip strength, agility, and body control through structured obstacle training. It is one of the most engaging and effective kids activities we offer.
Does my child need prior experience to start?
No experience is required for any program. All training begins with movement fundamentals. Whether your child is 3 years old or 17, we start with what they can do and build from there.
Is climbing safe for children?
Yes. All climbing sessions are coach-supervised with age-appropriate progressions and safety standards. Climbing is one of the most complete physical development activities available to young athletes: building grip, pulling strength, core stability, and spatial awareness simultaneously.
Can my child participate in more than one program?
Yes. Most dedicated athletes do. Combining the obstacle course, climbing wall, and Forge Protocol strength training creates a complete development system for kids. We will help you build the right weekly schedule based on age, goals, and recovery.
What equipment is in the facility?
Strata includes a full Ninja Warrior obstacle course, a climbing wall, and a strength training area with barbells, dumbbells, kettlebells, resistance bands, and bodyweight equipment, all available through coached classes and Open Mat.
How do I book a birthday party?
Birthday parties are private bookings, separate from the class schedule. Contact us through the contact page, mention the birthday party, and we will get availability and details to you quickly.
What is Open Gym and how much does it cost?
Open Gym is walk-in access to the obstacle course, climbing wall, and training area during scheduled Open Mat hours. It costs $20 online in advance, which also signs your waiver automatically, or $25 at the door. It is included free for current Strata members.
