Common Questions

Programs FAQ

What is the Forge Protocol?
The Forge Protocol is Strata's structured strength training system. It is built around the foundational movement patterns — squat, hinge, push, pull, carry — applied with progressive overload and a strict movement quality standard. Strength is earned through structure and repetition, not shortcuts.
Is the Ninja Warrior training just for kids?
No. The obstacle course system at Strata serves toddlers through teens. The progressions scale to meet each athlete where they are. Every age group benefits from developing coordination, grip strength, agility, and body control through structured obstacle training.
Do athletes need prior experience to start?
No experience is required for any program. All training begins with movement fundamentals. Whether someone is 6 years old or 45, we start with what they can do and build from there.
Is climbing safe for children?
Yes. All climbing sessions are coach-supervised with age-appropriate progressions and safety standards. Climbing is one of the most complete physical development activities available to young athletes — building grip, pulling strength, core stability, and spatial awareness simultaneously.
Can my child or I participate in more than one program?
Yes — and most serious athletes do. Combining the obstacle course, climbing wall, and Forge Protocol strength training creates a complete development system. We will help you build the right weekly schedule based on goals and recovery.
What equipment is in the facility?
Strata includes a full Ninja Warrior obstacle course, a climbing wall, and a strength training area with barbells, dumbbells, kettlebells, resistance bands, and bodyweight equipment — all available through coached classes and Open Mat.
How do I book a birthday party?
Birthday parties are private bookings, separate from the class schedule. Contact us through the contact page, mention the birthday party, and we will get availability and details to you quickly.
