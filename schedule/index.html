Quick Answers

Schedule FAQ

How do I sign up for a class?
Book your first free trial class using the button on this page. After your trial, we'll get you set up with the right membership for your schedule.
Can my child try more than one program?
Absolutely. Many of our athletes attend multiple classes per week. We'll recommend the right combination based on their goals and age.
What should my child wear?
Comfortable athletic wear and athletic shoes. For climbing sessions, we'll let you know about footwear when you book.
What if a class is full?
Contact us and we'll add your athlete to the waitlist or find an equivalent time slot that works.
How long are classes?
Most group classes are 60 minutes. Open Mat sessions are 90–120 minutes depending on the day.
Are birthday party bookings on this schedule?
Birthday parties are private bookings on weekends. Use the contact form and we'll get availability details to you quickly.

Ready to get on the schedule?

Your first class is free. Pick a time and let's get started.
