Our Story

Oroville's Youth Climbing,
Obstacle & Strength Facility

Strata Strength Lab is a youth climbing, obstacle, movement, and strength development facility in downtown Oroville, built on 20+ years of coaching young athletes across Butte County. Coach Darrell Thompson developed the Forge Protocol to fill a gap he kept seeing: kids who were active but untrained, and programs that didn't hold their attention long enough to build anything real.

If your child has tried leagues, classes, or other activities and didn't stick, this is a different environment entirely. Youth climbing and Ninja Warrior-style obstacles aren't workouts kids dread. They're challenges kids come back for. The strength, confidence, and coordination build in the process.

FAQ

Common Questions Answered

What is Strata Strength Lab?
Strata Strength Lab is a youth climbing, obstacle, movement, and strength development facility in downtown Oroville, CA. We offer Ninja Warrior-style obstacle training, a climbing wall, and the Forge Protocol structured strength system for kids and teens ages 1 to 18. This is not a traditional gym. It is a great option for families looking for fun, active, structured kids activities in Oroville where children build strength, confidence, coordination, and real-world capability through coach-led challenges.
What if my child has never stayed with a program before?
Most of the kids who come to Strata have tried other activities: leagues, gym classes, martial arts. And didn't stick. The difference here is the environment. Obstacle training and climbing are inherently engaging. Kids don't feel like they're working out. They feel like they're conquering something. Strata is great for kids who need a healthy outlet, want to improve in sports, or simply need something active and fun to do. The discipline, strength, and confidence build in the process.
How do I get my child started?
Book a free trial class through our website or give us a call. We will get your child into the right age group and program track so they can jump into a real class and see what Strata is about.
What is the Forge Protocol?
The Forge Protocol is Strata's structured strength training system. Built around foundational movement patterns: squat, hinge, push, pull, carry. With progressive overload and a strict movement quality standard. Strength earned through structure, not shortcuts.
What age groups do you serve?
Strata serves ages 1 through teens. Our Little Builders program is designed for ages 1 to 4 with age-appropriate movement development. From there, we group athletes by age and ability all the way through high school so every athlete trains at the right level.
Is strength training safe for kids?
Yes. When properly coached, youth strength training is one of the safest and most effective forms of physical development available to children. Our coaches are trained in age-appropriate programming and movement standards.
Do athletes need prior experience?
No. All programs start with movement fundamentals. Whether your child has never trained before or is a multi-sport athlete, we meet every athlete exactly where they are and build from there.
Where is Strata Strength Lab located?
We are located at 1726 Montgomery Street, Unit 8, in downtown Oroville, CA 95965. We serve kids and families across Oroville and Butte County, including Chico, Paradise, Gridley, and surrounding communities.
Do you offer birthday parties?
Yes. Strata hosts active, coach-led birthday parties for kids in Oroville. Private 2-hour events with obstacle course activities, climbing wall access, and relay games. A great alternative to bounce houses for families who want something exciting and active. Contact us to check weekend availability.
