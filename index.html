Our Story

Oroville's Strength &
Movement Training Facility

Strata Strength Lab is not a traditional gym. It is a development environment built around Ninja Warrior obstacle training, a climbing wall, and the Forge Protocol structured strength system — designed for young athletes from toddlers through teens who want to build real capability, not just go through the motions.

The name Strata reflects the method. Geology builds its strongest formations under pressure, over time, one layer on top of another. That is how we build athletes — with structure, repetition, and a standard that does not bend. Layer by layer. Rep by rep. Until it holds under anything.

FAQ

Common Questions Answered

What is Strata Strength Lab?
Strata Strength Lab is a youth strength and movement training facility in Oroville, CA. We offer Ninja Warrior obstacle training, climbing wall development, and the Forge Protocol structured strength system for toddlers through teens. This is not a traditional gym — it is a development environment built around real-world capability.
What is the Forge Protocol?
The Forge Protocol is Strata's structured strength training system. Built around foundational movement patterns — squat, hinge, push, pull, carry — with progressive overload and a strict movement quality standard. Strength earned through structure, not shortcuts.
What age groups do you serve?
Strata serves toddlers through teens — from our youngest athletes just starting to develop movement skills, all the way through high school age. Classes are grouped by age and ability so every athlete trains at the right level.
Is strength training safe for kids?
Yes. When properly coached, youth strength training is one of the safest and most effective forms of physical development available to children. Our coaches are trained in age-appropriate programming and movement standards.
Do athletes need prior experience?
No. All programs start with movement fundamentals. Whether your child has never trained before or is a multi-sport athlete, we meet every athlete exactly where they are and build from there.
Where is Strata Strength Lab located?
We are located at 1726 Montgomery Street, Unit 8, Oroville, CA 95965 — serving athletes and families across Oroville and Butte County.
Do you offer birthday parties?
Yes. Private 2-hour facility rentals with coached obstacle course activities, climbing wall access, and relay games. Contact us to check weekend availability.
