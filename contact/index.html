Contact FAQ

How do I book a free trial class?
Click the 'Book Free Trial Class' button on this page. It will take you to a short form where you pick a time that works for you. No payment or commitment required.
Where exactly is Strata Strength Lab located?
1726 Montgomery Street, Unit 8, Oroville, CA 95965. We are located in Oroville, California, serving families throughout Butte County.
Is there parking at the facility?
Yes, parking is available on-site. More specific details will be included in your booking confirmation.
How quickly will you respond to my message?
We typically respond within a few hours during business hours. If you need a faster response, booking a trial class directly is the quickest way to connect.
What are your hours?
Monday through Friday 3:00 PM – 7:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM. Closed Sundays. Hours may expand as we grow — check back or follow us on social media for updates.
Do I need to schedule a visit or can I just walk in?
We recommend booking in advance so we can be ready for your athlete. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours but a scheduled trial class ensures you get the coach's full attention.
